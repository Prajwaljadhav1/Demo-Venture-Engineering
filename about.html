About Venture

Structural engineering grounded in clear evidence.

We help owners, housing societies, developers, and industrial teams understand the condition of their structures and make sound decisions about what comes next.

Who We Are

A structural engineering and consultancy practice based in Pune.

Venture Civil and Consultancy Services works across the structural lifecycle: investigating existing buildings, testing materials, designing new structures, and planning repairs or strengthening.

Our work brings site observations, test results, engineering analysis, and practical documentation together so clients can understand the condition of a structure and the options available to them.

What We Do

From investigation to action.

  • Structural audits and stability certification
  • Concrete testing and condition assessment
  • Structural design and seismic analysis
  • Repair, strengthening, and technical documentation

Frequently Asked Questions

Key questions regarding legal audit requirements, testing methods, and turnaround times.

When is a Structural Audit mandatory by law?
Under section 353B of Municipal Corporation laws (including PMC, PCMC, and BMC regulations), any building older than 30 years must undergo a formal structural audit once every 3 years. Buildings between 15 to 30 years must be audited once every 5 years by a licensed structural engineer.
Does Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) damage our building?
No. Non-Destructive Tests (such as Rebound Hammer and Ultrasonic Pulse Velocity) operate on surface reflection and sound wave travel through concrete. They cause zero structural damage or cosmetic harm to your building.
What is included in a Structural Stability Certificate?
The dossier includes the statutory Form B signed by our empaneled structural engineer, complete NDT lab results, distress mapping photos, load-carrying capacity evaluations, and a prioritized repair methodology with bill of quantities (BOQ).
How long does a typical structural audit and report take?
The on-site field testing and visual inspection typically take 1 to 2 days depending on the built-up area. The complete analytical report and Stability Certificate are delivered within 5 to 7 business days.

MUNICIPAL FORM B

Structural Audit & Stability Certification

Assessment of an existing building’s condition, structural distress, and stability, with reports prepared for owners, societies, and municipal submissions.

Typical scope

  • Visual inspection and structural distress mapping
  • Concrete condition and reinforcement corrosion assessment
  • Deflection, settlement, and crack evaluation
  • Stability report, Form B certificate, and repair recommendations
Relevant standards: IS 456, IS 13311, NBC 2016, applicable municipal requirements
IS 516 / IS 13311

Non-Destructive Testing (NDT)

On-site testing to investigate concrete strength and uniformity while limiting damage to the structure.

Typical scope

  • Rebound Hammer testing for surface hardness
  • Ultrasonic Pulse Velocity (UPV) testing for internal quality
  • Cover meter checks for reinforcement location and concrete cover
  • Core extraction and laboratory testing when required
Relevant standards: IS 13311 Parts 1 and 2, IS 516
IS 456 / IS 1893

Structural Design & Seismic Analysis

Structural engineering for residential, commercial, and industrial projects, with coordinated calculations and drawings for site execution.

Typical scope

  • RCC frames, steel structures, industrial sheds, and PEBs
  • Seismic analysis and structural modeling
  • Foundation design based on project and soil information
  • Structural drawings, design reports, and reinforcement schedules
Relevant standards: IS 456, IS 1893, IS 800, IS 13920
IS 15988:2013

Repair & Seismic Retrofitting

Repair and strengthening recommendations based on the observed causes of structural distress and the performance needed from the building.

Typical scope

  • Repair plans for cracking, spalling, and reinforcement corrosion
  • RC column jacketing and micro-concrete specifications
  • Epoxy crack injection and grouting recommendations
  • CFRP strengthening options and tender documentation
Relevant standards: IS 15988:2013 and applicable repair guidance
IS 13920 DUCTILE DETAILING

CAD Detailing & Drafting

Clear structural drawings that translate design intent into practical instructions for contractors and site teams.

Typical scope

  • Reinforcement detailing for beams, columns, slabs, and joints
  • Bar Bending Schedules with cutting lengths
  • Structural steel fabrication and connection drawings
  • Coordination notes and drawing revisions
Relevant standards: IS 13920, SP 34, project design codes
LASER / TOTAL STATION

As-Built Drawings & Surveys

Measured documentation for existing buildings that need reliable drawings for renovation, assessment, or future planning.

Typical scope

  • Measured floor plans, column grids, and structural layouts
  • Beam, slab, and load-bearing wall documentation
  • Elevation and section measurements
  • CAD drawing and PDF handover
Deliverables: Digital measured drawings in agreed formats
BOQ & TENDER SUPPORT

Estimation, Costing & BOQ

Quantity and cost documentation to help owners and project teams plan repair or construction work and compare contractor proposals.

Typical scope

  • Item-wise Bills of Quantities and work descriptions
  • Rate analysis and repair budget estimates
  • Tender documentation and bid comparisons
  • Stage-wise quantity and billing review
Basis: Applicable PWD / CPWD schedules and project rates
